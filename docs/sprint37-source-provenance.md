# Sprint 37 Source and Provenance Policy

## Policy

Sprint 37 questions are original practice content. Research may confirm curriculum concepts, grade level, common misconceptions, and suitable question shapes. Research is not permission to reproduce a publisher, cram-school, school, or online question verbatim.

## Provenance classes

- `fully_original`: authored from the confirmed scope and project design without a source-specific prompt.
- `web_inspired_original`: public source informed the concept, difficulty, misconception, or shape; wording, values, context, options, and explanation are newly authored.
- `direct_reuse_licensed`: only allowed when the source explicitly permits reproduction; manifest must include URL, title, access date, license/reuse basis, and the exact reused portion.

## Required manifest fields

Every implemented entry records `provenanceClass`, `sourceUrl`, `sourceTitle`, `accessDate`, and `reuseBasis`. For `fully_original`, source fields are `null` and reuse basis states that no external text was reproduced. For `web_inspired_original`, the record explains what was learned and how the final item was independently authored.

## Copyright boundary

No bulk copying from commercial or publisher question banks. If a source has unclear reuse rights, use it only as background and write a self-contained original question. A copyright concern that cannot be resolved by original rewriting is a Human Exception and blocks only the affected candidate.
